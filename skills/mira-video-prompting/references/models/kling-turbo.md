## How it reads a prompt

MODEL: Kling 3.0 Turbo (Kuaishou) - the fast tier of Kling 3.0. The family's strengths at a lower price and a quicker render: photoreal people with clean anatomy, physically believable motion (weight, momentum, cloth, hair, water) and precise camera control. Text-to-video, or image-to-video from ONE start frame; native audio is always on. Write ONE continuous take - no shot list and no 'shot n, m' syntax: subject and appearance, the action as cause and effect, the setting, ONE camera move with its speed ('a slow push-in', 'the camera tracks her and freezes the instant she stops'), then light and grade. With a start frame, describe only what moves and how the camera moves - the frame already fixes subject, scene and style. Speech goes as Dialogue (speaker): "line", with the speaker's action BEFORE the line. 3-15 s.

## Doctrine

Pick it for drafts, volume and quick social clips with people; pick kling when the shot needs several reference photos, a reference clip, an end frame or a multi-shot sequence.
