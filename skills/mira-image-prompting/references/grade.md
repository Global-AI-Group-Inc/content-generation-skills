# Grades

GRADE - pick ONE and describe it as colour behaviour, not as a preset name: which hues sit in the shadows, which in the highlights, how deep the blacks go, how saturated the midtones are. A named stock may be mentioned once as a reference point, but the physical description is what actually steers the render.

### Grades

- **`portra_400`** — Warm forgiving skin, pastel highlights, gentle contrast, fine grain. The safest look for people.
- **`vision3_500t`** — Tungsten-balanced cinema stock, cool shadows against warm practicals, soft highlight roll-off.
- **`cinestill_800t`** — Tungsten film with red halation blooming around bright lights. Night, neon, wet streets.
- **`ektachrome`** — Slide film: clean saturated colour, snappy contrast, true blues. Travel and outdoor product.
- **`fuji_400h`** — Airy pastels, mint-leaning greens, low contrast. Weddings, skincare, soft lifestyle brands.
- **`ilford_hp5`** — Black and white, visible grain, full tonal range. Editorial portraiture.
- **`bleach_bypass`** — Desaturated with crushed contrast and a silvery sheen. Gritty, technical, sport.
- **`teal_orange`** — Warm skin against cool teal shadows. The mainstream commercial grade; effective and very familiar.
- **`nostalgic_amber`** — Amber highlights, lifted milky blacks, softened contrast. Memory and warmth.
- **`noir_neon`** — Deep blacks with saturated magenta and cyan sources. Nightlife, gaming, energy drinks.
- **`silver_retain`** — Near-monochrome with one colour surviving. Graphic and premium.
- **`indie_pastel`** — Muted desaturated palette, soft contrast, slightly green shadows. Small-batch and craft brands.
- **`honey_backlight`** — Golden backlight, warm haze, glowing edges. Food, bakery, anything appetising.
- **`steel_blue`** — Cool desaturated blues, neutral skin, clean whites. Tech, clinical, professional services.
- **`mono`** — Straight black and white, no colour cast, contrast doing all the work.
- **`cold_clinical`** — Neutral to cool, high key, no grade character at all. Medical, laboratory, technical documentation.
- **`clean_commercial`** — Accurate colour, no stylisation, pure white where white belongs. The correct choice for a marketplace card - a grade here is a defect.
